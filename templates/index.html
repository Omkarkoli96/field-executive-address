Loan Recovery Operations

Address Verification Tool

Upload your Customer Data and Visited Data files. Addresses are geocoded and compared automatically — visits within 1 km of the customer's address are marked Verified. The result downloads as an Excel file as soon as you submit.

Required columns: Loan_ID, Customer_Address (Customer_Name optional)

Required columns: Loan_ID, Visited_Address (Executive_Name, Visit_Date optional)

Processing — geocoding addresses can take a few seconds per unique address the first time. Your download will start automatically.
Each row in Visited Data is matched to Customer Data by Loan_ID. Rows are marked VERIFIED (≤1 km), NOT_VERIFIED (>1 km), COULD_NOT_GEOCODE (an address couldn't be located), or LOAN_ID_NOT_FOUND (no matching customer record).
